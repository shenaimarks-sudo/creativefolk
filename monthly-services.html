WHAT WE'LL NEED FROM YOU

Good social media management requires real, current content from your business. You'll need a usable collection of photos or video footage, or be willing to create and supply these assets as we work together.

We can provide ideas, prompts and scripts, but you or your team will need to capture new content regularly. Stock imagery can fill occasional gaps, but it cannot replace original content from your business.

If you don't currently have suitable assets, we may recommend arranging professional photography or videography before your service begins.
