ABOUT CREATIVE FOLK.

Creative Folk exists for business owners who are good at what they do, but have outgrown keeping everything in their head.

The work is good. The clients are happy. The business is growing. But somewhere along the way, things have started to feel messy. The content is happening when there is time. The systems are half-written, or not written at all. The team needs clearer direction. The marketing does not quite match how good the business actually is.

We help make sense of the messy middle. That might mean social media strategy, website copy, SEO support, a content plan, clearer messaging, onboarding documents, team resources, reporting, business systems or someone finally pulling all the moving pieces into a plan that makes sense.

We work with a variety of clients, but for all of them, our mission stays the same. We help make the business easier to understand, easier to trust and easier to run.

Hi, I’m Shenai.

I have always been the person who can look at a messy situation, find the pattern, and turn it into something people can actually use.

Before Creative Folk, I spent years inside big organisations working across operations, training, instructional design, management and process improvement. A lot of my work was about taking complicated things and making them clearer, easier to follow and easier to hand over.

Creative Folk started as SM Consulting when I moved to Thailand. At the time, it was just me taking on remote work and helping businesses with the things they did not have the time, headspace or internal support to fix.

Over time, the work grew into what Creative Folk is now: strategy, marketing, social media, SEO support, business systems, learning design, reporting and practical support for small businesses that need clear thinking and reliable hands behind the scenes.

NOT A BIG AGENCY.
NOT A ONE-PERSON BAND.

We are a small team of good folk who care about doing the work properly. We care about the creative, the strategy, the systems and the details that most people only notice when they are missing. The small things matter to us because they are usually the things that make a business feel easier to understand, easier to trust and easier to work with.

We are not here to throw more content at the wall and hope something sticks. We are not here to make your business sound like everyone else, or build systems your team will never use. Our work is practical, considered and built around what your business actually needs, not what looks impressive in a proposal.

We look at what is already happening, what your clients need to understand, and what needs to be made clearer so the business can run better from the inside and make more sense from the outside.

Clearer messaging

We help you understand how to communicate with your potential clients. We help those people understand what you do and why they should trust you, without making your business sound like a boring brochure.

Better systems

We turn the repeated, messy, stuck-in-your-head things into processes, checklists and resources your business can actually use. Our Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are easy to use and understand.

Smarter content

We create content that is planned and strategised for your Ideal Client. We write and design all of your content and report back each month. We share all KPIs and provide further recommendations to keep exceeding expectations.
